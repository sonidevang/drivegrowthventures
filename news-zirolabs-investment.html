Drive Growth Ventures Invests in ZiroLabs AI

Drive Growth Ventures today announced an investment in ZiroLabs AI, a company building software that turns a person's own health data, wearables, lab results, sleep and activity metrics, into insights they can understand and act on. The investment deepens the firm's focus on the intersection of healthcare and artificial intelligence.

The financing will support ZiroLabs as it grows its team, expands its product, and brings clear, trustworthy health intelligence to a wider audience. Drive Growth Ventures will work alongside the founders on hiring, go-to-market, and the operational foundations needed to scale responsibly in a regulated category.

ZiroLabs is tackling a problem we care deeply about: making personal health data genuinely useful to the people it belongs to. We're proud to partner with this team, and to back it with more than capital.

The investment reflects Drive Growth Ventures' focus on healthcare and artificial intelligence, two of the four sectors, alongside Space and FinTech, where the firm backs technical founders from pre-seed through Series B. Across every investment, the firm partners with founders as more than a line on the cap table, contributing capital, know-how, talent, go-to-market support, and access to its network.

Why this investment

Consumer health data is growing faster than the tools available to interpret it. ZiroLabs' approach, prioritizing comprehension and everyday decisions over raw dashboards, reflects a conviction Drive Growth Ventures shares: that the most valuable health products will be defined by the trust they earn, through clarity and results, rather than by the volume of data they collect.

About Drive Growth Ventures

Drive Growth Ventures is an early-stage venture capital firm investing in Healthcare, Space, AI & Cloud, and FinTech. The firm backs technical founders from the first check through Series B, pairing capital with hands-on support across hiring, go-to-market, regulatory strategy, follow-on fundraising, and partnerships. Learn more at drivegrowthventures.com.

About ZiroLabs AI

ZiroLabs AI builds consumer health-intelligence software that translates personal health data into clear, actionable insight. Learn more at zirolabs.ai.
