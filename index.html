For Founders

Built for the people doing the building.

Straight answers, before you even reach out.

What stage do you invest at?

Pre-seed through Series B. We lead and follow, and we re-up in the companies we believe in as they grow.

How fast do you move?

A first conversation within days, and a clear answer in weeks, not months. Your time is the scarcest thing you have.

Do you take board seats?

Only when it genuinely helps. We bring leverage, access, and hands-on work, never control for its own sake.

What do you look for?

Technical founders solving hard problems in Healthcare, Space, AI & Cloud, and FinTech, with an unfair insight into their market.

How do you help after the check?

Everything under "How We Help", from your first key hires to go-to-market to your next raise. Ask any founder we back.
